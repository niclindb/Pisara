Lesson 11 let you say what happened: **fui**, **vi**, **comí**. But a story also needs the background. "It was raining, the café was full, and I was reading" isn't a list of events; it's the scene the events happen in. Spanish gives that job to a second past tense, the **imperfect** (*imperfecto*). Once you have both, you can tell any story the way native speakers do.

**Warm-up (from lesson 11):** say "It was incredible" and "I saw the pyramids". The answers are at the end.

## The forms: the easiest tense in Spanish

| | **hablar** | **comer** | **vivir** |
|---|---|---|---|
| yo | habl**aba** | com**ía** | viv**ía** |
| tú | habl**abas** | com**ías** | viv**ías** |
| él, ella, usted | habl**aba** | com**ía** | viv**ía** |
| nosotros | habl**ábamos** | com**íamos** | viv**íamos** |
| ellos, ustedes | habl**aban** | com**ían** | viv**ían** |

Only **three** verbs are irregular:

- **ser**: era, eras, era, éramos, eran
- **ir**: iba, ibas, iba, íbamos, iban
- **ver**: veía, veías, veía, veíamos, veían

**Tener**, **hacer**, **estar**, **querer** and **poder** are all regular here: **tenía**, **hacía**, **estaba**, **quería**, **podía**.

## When to use it

SpanishDict's [preterite vs imperfect guide](https://www.spanishdict.com/guide/preterite-vs-imperfect-in-spanish) has a long list; for conversation, three uses cover most of it:

- **Background and description:** **Hacía sol. La playa estaba llena.** It was sunny. The beach was full.
- **What used to happen:** **De niño, vivía en Ohio. Íbamos al lago cada verano.** As a child I lived in Ohio. We used to go to the lake every summer.
- **Time and age in the past:** **Eran las diez. Tenía veinte años.** It was ten o'clock. I was twenty.

## Background versus event

The two tenses work as a pair. Picture a film: the imperfect is the camera showing the scene, and the preterite is something happening in it.

- **Estaba en el café cuando sonó mi teléfono.** I was in the café when my phone rang. Being there is the scene (estaba); the phone ringing is the event (sonó).
- **Llovía, así que fuimos al museo.** It was raining, so we went to the museum.

A quick test: if you could add "used to" or "was …-ing" in English, it's probably the imperfect. If it's a single completed thing that moves the story forward, it's the preterite.

## The same verb, both ways

Some verbs shift meaning with the tense:

- **Quería ir.** I wanted to go (a state of mind). / **Quise ir.** I tried to go.
- **Conocía a Ana.** I knew Ana. / **Conocí a Ana.** I met Ana (the moment I came to know her).
- **Era un viaje largo** describes the trip in the middle of the story. **Fue un viaje largo** sums it up once it's over.

## Try it today

- **Amiga:** ¿Cómo conociste a tu novia?
- **Tú:** Pues, era el verano de 2019. Yo vivía en Madrid y trabajaba en una empresa pequeña.
- **Tú:** Un sábado hacía mucho calor y fui a la piscina con unos amigos. Ella estaba allí con su hermana.
- **Tú:** Empezamos a hablar y… ¡hablamos tres horas!

**Warm-up answers:** **Fue increíble** and **Vi las pirámides**.

## Which sentence means "When I was a child, I used to play football"?

- Cuando fui niño, jugué al fútbol
- [x] Cuando era niño, jugaba al fútbol
- Cuando fui niño, jugaba al fútbol

A habit in the past and the description around it both take the imperfect: era, jugaba.

## "I was sleeping when the alarm went off." Which is right?

- [x] Dormía cuando sonó la alarma
- Dormí cuando sonaba la alarma
- Dormí cuando sonó la alarma

Sleeping is the background (dormía); the alarm is the single event that interrupts it (sonó).

## How do you say "It was ten o'clock" in a story?

- Fueron las diez
- [x] Eran las diez
- Estaban las diez

Clock time uses ser (lesson 6), and as background in the past it's the imperfect: eran.

## Problem

Tell the short story of a memorable meal, in five or six sentences. Set the scene with at least three imperfect verbs (where you were, what the weather or place was like, how you felt), then tell what happened with at least three preterite verbs.

## Solution

One version:

> Era mi primer día en Lisboa. Hacía mucho frío y yo tenía mucha hambre. El restaurante estaba casi vacío. Pedí la sopa del día y el camarero me recomendó un vino de la casa. Fue la mejor cena del viaje.

What to check in yours:

- **Scene in the imperfect:** era, hacía, tenía, estaba.
- **Events in the preterite:** pedí, recomendó, fue.
- **Fue la mejor cena** sums up the whole meal once it's over, so preterite. **Era la mejor cena** would sound like the story is still going.
- **Pedí** from **pedir**: regular in the yo preterite, though in the él form it's **pidió**.
