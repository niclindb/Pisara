A practical start in Spanish for travel and everyday life. Each lesson gives you a handful of phrases you can use the same day, how to say them, and the one or two grammar points that make them work. No long verb tables: just enough to greet people, order food, find your way, handle numbers and times, and keep a short conversation going.

The phrases work across Spain and Latin America. Where a country says something differently, the lesson points it out.

<!-- outline -->

1. Greetings and introductions (Beginner): hello and goodbye, your name and where you're from, tú or usted
2. Ordering food and drinks (Beginner): getting a table, asking for what you want, paying the bill
3. Asking for directions (Beginner): where is…, left, right and straight on, understanding the answer
4. Numbers, prices and time (Beginner): counting to 100, asking how much, telling the time
5. Everyday small talk (Beginner): the weather, what you like, your plans, keeping a conversation going
