You're back from a week in Mexico City and a friend asks **"¿Qué tal el viaje?"** To answer you need "I went", "we did", "I had", "it was", "I saw". Every one of those is irregular in the Spanish preterite. The same pattern from lesson 8 holds: the verbs people use most are the ones that break the rules. The good news is that they break them in a few shared ways.

**Warm-up (from lesson 10):** say "I arrived yesterday" and "We ate at a restaurant". The answers are at the end.

## Ir and ser: one past for both

**Ir** (go) and **ser** (be) have the same preterite:

| yo | tú | él, ella, usted | nosotros | ellos, ustedes |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **fui** | fuiste | fue | fuimos | fueron |

Context sorts it out. **Fui a Oaxaca** is "I went to Oaxaca" (a place, so ir). **Fue increíble** is "It was incredible" (a description, so ser).

## The new-stem verbs

A group of common verbs swap in a new stem and then all share one set of endings, with **no written accents**: **-e, -iste, -o, -imos, -ieron**.

| verb | new stem | yo | él, ella |
|---|---|---|---|
| **estar** (be) | estuv- | estuve | estuvo |
| **tener** (have) | tuv- | tuve | tuvo |
| **poder** (can) | pud- | pude | pudo |
| **querer** (want) | quis- | quise | quiso |
| **venir** (come) | vin- | vine | vino |
| **hacer** (do) | hic- | hice | **hizo** |
| **decir** (say) | dij- | dije | dijo |

Two small quirks: **hizo** is spelled with a z to keep its sound, and stems ending in **j** drop the i in the plural: **dijeron**, not *dijieron*.

## Ver and dar: tiny and regular-ish

**Ver** (see): **vi, viste, vio, vimos, vieron**. **Dar** (give): **di, diste, dio, dimos, dieron**. One syllable each, so they need no accent marks.

## Estuve or fui?

Both can translate "I was". **Estuve en Madrid tres días** is "I was in Madrid for three days": being somewhere is location, which takes estar (lesson 6). **Fue un viaje largo** is "It was a long trip": describing what something was takes ser. Lesson 6's rule still decides.

## Linking the story

These small words turn a list into a story:

- **primero** first, **luego** / **después** then, afterwards, **al final** in the end
- **por la mañana / tarde / noche** in the morning, afternoon, at night
- **el primer día** on the first day, **el último día** on the last day

## Try it today

- **Amigo:** ¿Qué tal el viaje a México?
- **Tú:** ¡Fue increíble! Estuve una semana en la Ciudad de México.
- **Amigo:** ¿Y qué hiciste?
- **Tú:** El primer día fui a Teotihuacán y vi las pirámides. Luego tuve un problema con el hotel, pero al final pude cambiar de habitación.
- **Amigo:** ¿Y la comida?
- **Tú:** Comí tacos todos los días. Mi amigo vino el jueves y fuimos juntos a Coyoacán.

**Warm-up answers:** **Llegué ayer** (the g gets a u) and **Comimos en un restaurante**.

## How do you say "We went to the beach"?

- [x] Fuimos a la playa
- Íbamos a la playa
- Vamos a la playa

Fuimos is the preterite of ir. Vamos is present, and íbamos is a different past you'll meet in lesson 13.

## How do you say "What did you do?" to a friend?

- ¿Qué haciste?
- [x] ¿Qué hiciste?
- ¿Qué hacías tú?

Hacer takes the stem hic- in the preterite: hice, hiciste, hizo.

## Which sentence means "I couldn't sleep"?

- No podí dormir
- [x] No pude dormir
- No puedé dormir

Poder becomes pud- and takes the accentless ending -e: pude.

## Problem

Write five sentences about a real trip you took, for a friend who asks "¿Qué tal el viaje?" Use at least four different irregular verbs from this lesson, and at least two linking words.

## Solution

One version:

> Fue un viaje muy bonito. Estuve cinco días en Barcelona con mi novia. Primero fuimos a la Sagrada Familia, y luego vimos el mar desde Montjuïc. El último día tuve que trabajar un poco, pero no pasa nada. Al final, ¡no quise volver a casa!

What to check in yours:

- **Fue** for describing the trip, **fui/fuimos** for going: same forms, different verbs.
- **Estuve** for how long you were somewhere, not *fui*.
- No accents on **tuve**, **pude**, **hice**, **estuve**, and **hizo** with a z.
- **Vi**, **vio**: one syllable, no accent.
