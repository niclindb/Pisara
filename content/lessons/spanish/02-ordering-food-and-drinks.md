You can order a whole meal in Spanish with one pattern: **"Para mí, ___, por favor."** (For me, ___, please.) This lesson gives you that pattern plus the phrases for before and after it.

## Getting a table

- **Una mesa para dos, por favor.** A table for two, please. Swap in **uno, tres, cuatro**.
- **¿Tienen mesa libre?** Do you have a free table?
- **La carta, por favor.** The menu, please. (In Latin America you'll also hear **el menú**.)

## Ordering

The waiter will ask **¿Qué van a tomar?** or **¿Qué les pongo?** (What will you have?). Answer with any of these:

- **Para mí, la paella, por favor.** For me, the paella, please.
- **Quisiera un café con leche.** I'd like a coffee with milk. **Quisiera** (kee-SYEH-rah) is the polite "I would like".
- **¿Me trae una cerveza, por favor?** Could you bring me a beer?
- **¿Qué me recomienda?** What do you recommend?

**Un** or **una** means "a". Most nouns ending in *-o* take **un** (un vino, un café), and most ending in *-a* take **una** (una cerveza, una mesa). If you get it wrong, everyone still understands you.

## Useful words

| Spanish | English |
|---|---|
| **agua** (con gas / sin gas) | water (sparkling / still) |
| **vino tinto / blanco** | red / white wine |
| **una cerveza** | a beer |
| **un café solo** | an espresso |
| **la cuenta** | the bill |
| **sin** | without |
| **soy vegetariano / vegetariana** | I'm vegetarian |
| **soy alérgico / alérgica a…** | I'm allergic to… |

**Sin** is the most useful word on this list: **sin cebolla** (no onion), **sin gluten**, **sin hielo** (no ice).

## Paying

- **La cuenta, por favor.** The check, please. In Spain and much of Latin America, the waiter won't bring it until you ask, because rushing you is considered rude.
- **¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?** Can I pay by card?
- **Todo junto** (all together) or **por separado** (separately).

Tipping varies: rounding up or leaving 5 to 10% is normal in Spain; 10% is common in Mexico, and some restaurants add **propina** (tip) to the bill already, so check before adding more.

## Try it today

Read this out loud, then say it again with your own order:

- **Camarero:** Buenas noches. ¿Qué van a tomar?
- **Tú:** Para mí, una cerveza y los tacos, por favor.
- **Camarero:** Muy bien. ¿Algo más? (Anything else?)
- **Tú:** No, gracias. Bueno, agua sin gas también.

## How do you ask for a burger without onion?

- Una hamburguesa con cebolla
- [x] Una hamburguesa sin cebolla
- Una hamburguesa por cebolla

## Problem

You're at a café with a friend. Order, in Spanish: a coffee with milk for you, a sparkling water for your friend, and ask what the waiter recommends to eat. Then, at the end, ask for the bill and whether you can pay by card.

## Solution

One natural version:

- **Ordering:** "Para mí, un café con leche, y para mi amigo, un agua con gas, por favor. ¿Qué nos recomienda para comer?"
- **Paying:** "La cuenta, por favor. ¿Puedo pagar con tarjeta?"

Notes:

- **Nos** recomienda (recommend *to us*) because you're asking for both of you. "¿Qué me recomienda?" is fine too.
- It's **un agua**, not *una agua*, even though agua is feminine. Words that start with a stressed *a* sound take **un** in the singular so the two *a* sounds don't run together. You won't be misunderstood either way.
- For a female friend, say **para mi amiga**.
