AI shows up everywhere now, from the photos app on your phone to the chatbots that write emails. This path explains what's really going on inside, in plain words and without math. You'll learn how machines learn from examples, what a neural network is, how a chatbot writes one word at a time, why it sometimes makes things up, and how to get better answers from it.

<!-- outline -->

1. What counts as AI (Beginner): rules vs. learning, narrow AI, where you already meet it
2. How machines learn from examples (Beginner): data, labels, training, testing on new examples
3. Neural networks without the math (Beginner): layers, weights, learning by small nudges
4. How a chatbot writes (Beginner): tokens, predicting the next token, sampling
5. From text predictor to assistant (Intermediate): pretraining, fine-tuning, feedback from people
6. Why AI makes things up (Intermediate): hallucinations, cutoff dates, checking answers
7. Getting good answers (Intermediate): context, examples, asking for steps, iterating
8. Attention: the idea behind transformers (Advanced): context windows, attention, why it scaled
